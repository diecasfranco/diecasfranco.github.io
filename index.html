About Me

I am a microbiologist specialising in host-associated microbiome research, with a focus on the honeybee gut microbiome and its role in bee health, nutrition, and response to agricultural management.

My work combines field experiments, 16S amplicon sequencing, and custom bioinformatics pipelines to understand how diet, environment, and colony management shape microbial communities in Apis mellifera.

I develop open-source Python tools for interactive analysis and visualisation of microbiome data, making complex multi-sample results accessible through self-contained HTML reports.
